# Meridian Cloud Tiers

Meridian Cloud is the hosted offering. Three tiers are available.

## Free

- Retention: 7 days
- Storage: 1 GB
- Rate limit: 1000 requests per minute
- Price: free

## Pro

- Retention: 90 days
- Storage: 100 GB
- Rate limit: 10,000 requests per minute
- Price: $49 per month

## Enterprise

- Retention: configurable
- Storage: custom
- Includes SSO, point-in-time recovery, and dedicated support
- Price: contact sales

Retention on the Free tier cannot be extended; upgrade to Pro or Enterprise for
longer retention.
